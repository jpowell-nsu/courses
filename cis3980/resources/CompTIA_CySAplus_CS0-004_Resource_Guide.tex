\section*{About This Guide}
This guide collects resources for the CompTIA Cybersecurity Analyst (CySA+) portion of the course. CySA+ is an intermediate certification that builds on Security+, so this list includes a note on the exam version change, official materials, free study resources, hands-on practice, books, practice tests, and community support. Each entry includes a one-line description and a link.

\section{Exam Snapshot}
CompTIA launched CySA+ V4 (exam code CS0-004) on June 23, 2026. The previous version, CS0-003, is being phased out, with the English-language exam retiring December 22, 2026. Students starting preparation now should study CS0-004 material. The table below summarizes the four domains measured on CS0-004 and their weight.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{CS0-004 exam domains and weighting}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.72\linewidth}r@{}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{navy}
\textcolor{white}{\textbf{Domain}} & \textcolor{white}{\textbf{Weight}} \\
\midrule
1.0 Security Operations & 34\% \\
\rowcolor{lightrow} 2.0 Vulnerability Management & 26\% \\
3.0 Incident Response and Management & 24\% \\
\rowcolor{lightrow} 4.0 Reporting and Communication & 16\% \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The exam allows a maximum of 85 multiple-choice and performance-based questions in 165 minutes, and requires a scaled score of 750 out of 900 to pass. CompTIA recommends roughly four years of hands-on experience as a SOC or vulnerability analyst, or Security+ plus equivalent experience, before attempting CySA+.
