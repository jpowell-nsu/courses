\section*{About This Guide}
This guide collects free and paid resources for the CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) portion of the course. Items are grouped by type so that a study plan can pull from each category: official CompTIA materials, video instruction, hands-on practice, books, practice tests, flashcards, and community support. Each entry includes a one-line description and a link.

\section{Exam Snapshot}
The table below summarizes the five domains measured on the SY0-701 exam and their approximate weight, based on CompTIA's published exam objectives.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{SY0-701 exam domains and weighting}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.72\linewidth}r@{}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{navy}
\textcolor{white}{\textbf{Domain}} & \textcolor{white}{\textbf{Weight}} \\
\midrule
1.0 General Security Concepts & 12\% \\
\rowcolor{lightrow} 2.0 Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations & 22\% \\
3.0 Security Architecture & 18\% \\
\rowcolor{lightrow} 4.0 Security Operations & 28\% \\
5.0 Security Program Management and Oversight & 20\% \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The exam allows a maximum of 90 multiple-choice and performance-based questions in 90 minutes, and requires a scaled score of 750 out of 900 to pass.

\end{itemize}
